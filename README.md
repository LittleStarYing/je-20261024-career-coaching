# Awesome-AI-Career-Coaching

## Top AI Career Coaching Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on AI Resume Tailoring, Interview Prep, Job Search Agents & Career Advancement Tools*

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **AI Career Coaching**. These tools help job seekers tailor resumes to job descriptions, prepare for interviews, track applications, research companies, and automate parts of the job-search process with generative AI.



**Examples** include Teal, Huntr, Jobscan, Resume Worded, Careerflow, Kickresume, Enhancv, Rezi.ai, Cultivated Culture, and Teal+ (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: AI career tools are increasingly available as open, local-first projects. **NextRole**, **career-ops**, and related multi-agent systems enable resume tailoring, interview prep, and job search assistance without proprietary lock-in. This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Teal / Teal+](https://www.tealhq.com/)**  

  Popular AI-powered career platform for resume building, job tracking, keyword matching, and interview preparation.



- **[Huntr](https://huntr.co/)**  

  Job application tracker and AI resume/cover-letter tools designed to keep the search organized and tailored.



- **[Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/)**  

  ATS-focused resume optimization tool that scores resumes against job descriptions and suggests keyword improvements.



- **[Resume Worded](https://resumeworded.com/)**  

  AI resume and LinkedIn review platform offering targeted feedback and score-based improvement suggestions.



- **[Careerflow](https://www.careerflow.ai/)**  

  AI career coach and job-search platform covering resume optimization, networking, and application tracking.



- **[Kickresume](https://www.kickresume.com/)**  

  AI resume and cover-letter builder with templates and content suggestions for job seekers.



- **[Enhancv](https://enhancv.com/)**  

  Modern resume builder focused on storytelling, design, and AI-assisted content tailored to roles.



- **[Rezi.ai](https://www.rezi.ai/)**  

  AI resume builder and job-search toolkit emphasizing ATS compatibility and keyword optimization.



- **[Cultivated Culture](https://cultivatedculture.com/)**  

  Career resources and tools (including AI-assisted resume and LinkedIn guidance) popular among job seekers.



- **[Teal+](https://www.tealhq.com/)**  

  Extended Teal offering with deeper AI features for resume tailoring, tracking, and career coaching workflows.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[NextRole](https://github.com/tam159/next-role)**  

  Open-source AI career assistant that tailors resumes to any job description, automates company research, and generates custom interview-prep plans via a multi-agent system with long-term memory.



- **[career-ops](https://github.com/santifer/career-ops)**  

  Open-source AI job-search agent that scans boards, scores jobs against your CV, tailors ATS-friendly resumes and cover letters, provides interview prep, and tracks applications — runs locally in AI coding CLIs.



- **[ai-job-search](https://github.com/MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search)**  

  Open-source AI job application framework (built around Claude Code and similar) for evaluating postings, tailoring CVs, writing cover letters, and preparing for interviews.



- **[GENAI-CareerAssistant-Multiagent](https://github.com/amanv1906/GENAI-CareerAssistant-Multiagent)**  

  Multi-agent GenAI career assistant for personalized job search, cover-letter generation, company research, and resume analysis.



- **[Lujie CareerKit](https://github.com/Chozzc/Lujie-Careerkit)**  

  Open-source AI resume builder and job tracker with role-specific tailoring, interview prep, and mock-interview support.



- **[JobFit AI](https://github.com/adeebhh/JobFit-AI)**  

  Privacy-first open-source resume analyzer that compares resumes to job descriptions using local AI (Ollama) for skill-gap and evidence analysis.



- **[RenderCV](https://github.com/rendercv/rendercv)**  

  Open-source resume generation toolkit often used by agent systems to produce clean, editable PDF resumes from structured data.



- **[Community multi-agent career pipelines](https://github.com/search?q=ai+resume+tailoring+agent)**  

  Growing set of open projects that combine LLM agents for resume rewriting, JD matching, and interview coaching.



- **[Local LLM + resume templates](https://github.com/search?q=resume+builder+ollama)**  

  Projects that pair local models with resume templates for fully offline or self-hosted career tooling.



- **[Documentation and ethical job-search agent guides](https://github.com/santifer/career-ops)**  

  Project docs and community discussions on building transparent, user-controlled AI career assistants.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Full multi-agent resume + interview pipelines with **NextRole** or **career-ops**.

- Local-first job search and application assistance that keeps data on your machine.

- Combining open resume generators (**RenderCV**) with LLM agents for tailored PDFs.

- Using privacy-focused analyzers (**JobFit AI**) with local models.

- Accepting that commercial platforms (Teal, Jobscan, Rezi, Kickresume, etc.) still lead in polished UX, large template libraries, and integrated job boards.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Run **NextRole** or **career-ops** locally → feed your CV + JD → generate tailored resume, company research, and interview battlecards → track applications in your own system. Suitable for privacy-conscious users and developers who want full control. Many job seekers still prefer commercial AI career platforms for convenience and design quality.
